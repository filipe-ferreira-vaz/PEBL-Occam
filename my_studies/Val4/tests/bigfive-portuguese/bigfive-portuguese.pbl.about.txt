PEBL implementation of the 44-item Big Five Inventory (BFI-44), European
Portuguese version ("Como sou eu?"), with subscales for extraversion,
agreeableness, conscientiousness, neuroticism and openness.
The test always runs in Portuguese (translations/bigfive-portuguese.pbl-pt.json).

See:  John, O. P., Donahue, E. M., & Kentle, R. L. (1991). The Big Five
      Inventory--Versions 4a and 54. Berkeley, CA: University of California,
      Berkeley, Institute of Personality and Social Research.
      Simoes, V. J. (2016). Adaptacao e Validacao do Teste de Personalidade
      Mini-IPIP e Big Five Inventory (BFI) a adultos portugueses.

Parameters allow you to randomize the order in which questions appear, and to select which of five subscales you wish to administer.
