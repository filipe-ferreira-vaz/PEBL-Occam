Task survey ("O que sinto sobre esta tarefa?") to give right after the
ptrails task. European Portuguese; always runs in Portuguese
(translations/survey-ptrails.pbl-pt.json).

Four questions about the task just done, each answered by clicking a box
from 1 to 7. Only boxes 1 and 7 have a text label (e.g. 1 = Muito difícil,
7 = Nada difícil); boxes 2-6 show only the number.
  q1 difficulty, q2 frustration, q3 interest, q4 enjoyment
  (1 = very ..., 7 = not ... at all)

Data: data/<code>/survey-ptrails-<code>.csv (one row per question),
      data/<code>/survey-ptrails-report-<code>.txt,
      data/survey-ptrails-pooled.csv (one row per participant: q1-q4, rt1-rt4).

Parameter: shufflequestions (0/1, default 0) randomizes the question order.
